About this site

This website holds leaflets from the speech and language therapy team, so you can read them on your phone or save them to keep.

Your privacy

  • This site does not ask for your name or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no log-ins, no cookies and no tracking.
  • QR codes and web links for leaflets contain leaflet codes only, never anything about you.
  • If you open leaflets, a list of them is kept in the browser on your own phone so you can find them again. It is not sent anywhere. You can clear it on the home page.
  • Like all websites, the company that hosts this site keeps standard technical records, such as internet (IP) addresses, to keep the service secure.

Accessibility

We want as many people as possible to be able to use this website. You should be able to:

  • make text larger using your phone or browser settings
  • use the site with a screen reader or keyboard only
  • use the site on older phones and slow connections

This is a demonstration site. A full accessibility statement will be published before it is used with patients.

Known issue: the demonstration leaflets are not set up for screen readers. Real leaflets will be checked before they are added.

If you need a leaflet in another format, such as large print, easy read or audio, please ask your speech and language therapist.
