    Keep this library on your phone

    iPhone or iPad
    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    Android phone or tablet
    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the menu button (three dots in the top corner).
    3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

    The library then opens like an app. Leaflets you have opened can usually be read again without signal.
